Employee Turnover Prediction at Salifort Motors

Business Problem

Salifort Motors is experiencing a high employee turnover rate, increasing operational costs and disrupting productivity. This project uses data analytics and machine learning to predict employee departure and identify key drivers of turnover.

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 14,999 employee records with satisfaction, workload, promotion history, and departmental information.

Key Insights from EDA

  • Employees with low satisfaction levels are significantly more likely to leave.
  • High average monthly hours correlate with increased attrition.
  • Lack of promotion is a strong predictor of turnover.

Modeling Approach

Two models were built:

  • Logistic Regression – primary model for interpretability
  • Random Forest – secondary model for performance comparison

Results

Logistic regression achieved strong ROC-AUC performance and clearly identified satisfaction, workload, and promotion history as the most important predictors.

Business Recommendations

  • Monitor employee satisfaction regularly
  • Balance workloads to prevent burnout
  • Create clearer promotion pathways

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how ethical, interpretable analytics can support workforce retention strategies and inform leadership decision-making.
